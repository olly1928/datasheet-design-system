## Constraints for whoever drafts it

The footer strip is sized for roughly **two lines at 7.6px** across the full content
width — about **340 characters**. The validator enforces that limit.

If Legal's wording runs longer, do not trim it to fit: raise it. The footer height is
one value in `template/datasheet.html` (`--foot-h`) and the strip can be made taller.
The wording is the constraint; the layout bends around it.
